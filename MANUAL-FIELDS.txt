KUWAPANI — MANUAL SETUP FIELDS

In the Excel workbook:
- YELLOW cells = enter your real business/product/order information.
- BLUE cells = formulas; do not overwrite unless you intend to change the calculation.

In .env.local and Vercel Environment Variables:
- Every [ADD_...] / [ADD ...] placeholder must be replaced with a value copied from the correct provider dashboard.
- Never share or commit Stripe secret keys or Supabase service-role keys.

In Supabase products table:
- Enter real product names, SKU, description, price_cents, stock_quantity, image_url and is_active.
- price_cents is cents: $10.00 = 1000.
